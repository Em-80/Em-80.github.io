Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Dice Minima is designed to be a simple, privacy-conscious digital dice roller.

Information We Collect

Dice Minima does not require an account and does not directly collect, store, or transmit personal information to Dice Minima servers.

Dice Minima does not operate user databases or backend servers for storing user information.

Advertising

Dice Minima displays advertisements using Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain information necessary to provide, measure, secure, and personalize advertising. This may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, diagnostic information, interactions with advertisements, and other information related to ad delivery.

The collection and use of this information is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and advertising policies.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Dice Minima uses Google's User Messaging Platform to provide privacy and advertising consent choices.

Users may be able to choose between personalized, non-personalized, limited, or other forms of advertising depending on their region and applicable privacy regulations.

When required, users can revisit their privacy choices from within Dice Minima.

Location Information

Dice Minima does not request or collect precise location information.

Advertising providers may determine approximate location using information such as an IP address for purposes including advertising, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

Accounts and User Content

Dice Minima does not require user accounts.

Dice rolls and ordinary application activity are not uploaded to Dice Minima servers.

Data Retention

Because Dice Minima does not operate servers that collect personal information, Dice Minima does not maintain a server-side database of personal user information.

Information processed by Google or other advertising providers is subject to their respective retention policies.

Children's Privacy

Dice Minima is a general-purpose tabletop gaming utility and is not designed specifically for children.

Advertising and privacy settings are configured in accordance with applicable Google Play and advertising requirements.

Third-Party Services

Dice Minima currently uses Google AdMob and related Google advertising services.

Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Dice Minima's features, advertising providers, or legal requirements change.

The current version of this policy will be available on the Dice Minima website.
